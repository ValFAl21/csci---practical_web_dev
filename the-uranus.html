Here Is Some Information About The Uranus Planet

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and is known as an ice giant because it is made mostly of water, ammonia, and methane ices, along with hydrogen and helium. It has a pale blue-green color because methane in its atmosphere absorbs the red light and reflects it as blue-green light. Uranus has a very cold atmosphere, faint rings, and 28 known moons. It takes approximately 84 Earth years to complete one orbit around the Sun.

5 Fun Facts About The Uranus Planet

1. Uranus rotates on its side. Uranus has an extreme tilt of about 98 degrees, making it look like it rolls around the Sun instead of spinning upright like most planets.

2. Uranus is extremely cold! Uranus has recorded atmospheric temperatures as low as -224°C (-371°F), making it the coldest planetary atmosphere measured in our Solar System.

3. Uranus has rings! Uranus has 13 known rings surrounding it. Unlike Saturn's bright rings, Uranus's rings are dark and difficult to see.

4. A year on Uranus lasts 84 Earth years! Because Uranus is so far from the Sun, it takes approximately 84 Earth years to complete one trip around it.

5. Uranus has unusual seasons! Because of its extreme tilt, each pole experiences approximately 42 years of continuous sunlight followed by 42 years of darkness during its long orbit around the Sun.
