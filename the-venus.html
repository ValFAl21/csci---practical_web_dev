Here Is Some Information About The Venus Planet

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and is similar in size and structure to Earth, which is why it is sometimes called Earth's "sister planet." However, Venus has a very thick atmosphere made mostly of carbon dioxide, making it the hottest planet in our Solar System. Venus is covered by thick clouds of sulfuric acid and has a rocky surface with mountains, volcanoes, and large plains. It takes about 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun.

5 Fun Facts About The Venus Planet

1. Venus is the hottest planet! Even though Mercury is closer to the Sun, Venus is hotter because its thick atmosphere traps heat through a powerful greenhouse effect.

2. A day on Venus is longer than a year! Venus takes about 243 Earth days to rotate once, but only about 225 Earth days to orbit the Sun.

3. Venus rotates backward! Venus rotates in the opposite direction from most planets. On Venus, the Sun would appear to rise in the west and set in the east.

4. Venus has no moons. Venus is one of only two planets in our Solar System without a natural moon. Mercury is the other.

5. Venus is sometimes called Earth's twin. Venus and Earth are similar in size, mass, and rocky composition, but their environments are extremely different. Venus is much hotter and has a thick, toxic atmosphere.
