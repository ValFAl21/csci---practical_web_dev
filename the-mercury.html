Here Is Some Information About The Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and is the only planet currently known to support life. It has a rocky surface, a protective atmosphere, and large amounts of liquid water. About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by water. Earth takes about 365 days to orbit the Sun and about 24 hours to rotate once on its axis. It has one natural satellite: the Moon.

5 Fun Facts About The Earth

1. Earth is mostly covered in water! About 71% of Earth's surface is covered by oceans, seas, and other bodies of water.

2. Earth isn't a perfect sphere. It is slightly flattened at the poles and wider around the equator.

3. Earth has an atmosphere that protects life. Our atmosphere helps provide oxygen, regulates temperature, and protects Earth from much of the Sun's harmful radiation.

4. Earth is constantly moving! Earth rotates on its axis while simultaneously orbiting the Sun at an average speed of about 107,000 km/h (67,000 mph).

5. Earth is the only known planet with life. Scientists have discovered thousands of planets outside our Solar System, but Earth is currently the only planet confirmed to have life.
