Here Is Some Information About The Moon

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. It orbits Earth and takes about 27.3 days to complete one orbit. The Moon does not produce its own light—it reflects light from the Sun. The Moon's gravity affects Earth, especially our ocean tides. It also has a rocky, dusty surface covered with many craters formed by impacts from space objects.

5 Fun Facts About The Moon

1. The Moon has no light of its own. The Moon looks bright because it reflects sunlight toward Earth.

2. People have walked on the Moon! The first humans landed on the Moon during Apollo 11 in 1969. A total of 12 astronauts have walked on the Moon.

3. The Moon is slowly moving away from Earth. It moves away from Earth by about 3.8 centimeters (1.5 inches) each year.

4. You always see the same side of the Moon. The Moon rotates once on its axis in about the same amount of time it takes to orbit Earth. This is called tidal locking.

5. The Moon has much weaker gravity than Earth. Gravity on the Moon is about one-sixth of Earth's gravity, which is why astronauts can make those famous giant-looking jumps!
