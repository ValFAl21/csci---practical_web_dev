Here Is Some Information About The Sun

The Sun is the star located at the center of our Solar System. It is a massive ball of extremely hot gas, made mostly of hydrogen and helium. Its gravity keeps the planets, moons, asteroids, and other objects in orbit around it. The Sun provides the light and heat that make life on Earth possible. It is approximately 4.6 billion years old and is the largest object in our Solar System.

Here Are Some Amazing Facts About The Sun!

1. The Sun is HUGE! About 1.3 million Earths could fit inside the Sun by volume.

2. The Sun is extremely hot! It's surface is about 5,500°C (10,000°F), while its core reaches roughly 15 million°C (27 million°F).

3. Sunlight takes about 8 minutes to reach Earth. The Sun is about 150 million kilometers (93 million miles) away from Earth.

4. The Sun is a star. It may look much bigger and brighter than other stars because it is much closer to Earth.

5. The Sun won't shine forever. In about 5 billion years, the Sun is expected to run out of the hydrogen fuel in its core and eventually become a white dwarf after passing through later stages of its life.
